Everything begins with an Idea. Cultivating the Next Generation of Growth, where Tradition finds Technology. We act as an incubator, fostering promising ventures with the potential to revolutionize through expert guidance, financial support, and a collaborative environment.

About a-IDEA, ICAR-NAARM

The Association for Innovation Development of Entrepreneurship in Agriculture (a-IDEA), is a Technology Business Incubator (TBI) hosted by ICAR-NAARM, Hyderabad. The a-IDEA has been fostering innovation and entrepreneurship in agriculture in India. The activities of a-IDEA are financially supported by different schemes of DST, DBT, NABARD, SIDBI, MSME, MeitY, and DPIIT.

Krishiboot 10.0 (Application Open Now)

KRISHIBOOT is an incubation programme of a-IDEA, ICAR-NAARM, Hyderabad, designed to support agriculture and allied-sector startups, particularly ventures at the ideation and early-startup stages. The programme helps promising innovators develop their ideas into scalable agri-businesses through structured incubation support. Last date to apply : 28 October 2026

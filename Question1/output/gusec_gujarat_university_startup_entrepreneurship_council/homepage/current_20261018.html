Welcome to GUSEC

Gujarat University Startup and Entrepreneurship Council (GUSEC) is one of India's leading startup support systems, which has set an unprecedented benchmark by facilitating at zero-cost. Right in the heart of Gujarat University, with an ever-growing infrastructure, 100+ innovative tech and non-tech startups, GUSEC has played a pivotal role in shaping and nurturing the startup ecosystem in Gujarat.

STARTUP ECOSYSTEM

Empowering Tomorrow’s Entrepreneurs

The Gujarat University Startup and Entrepreneurship Council (GUSEC) is an institutional pioneer in India's startup ecosystem. Operating directly within Gujarat University, GUSEC eliminates early financial and infrastructural barriers to empower raw ideas into scalable commercial enterprises.

By providing access to state-of-the-art labs, creative collaborative workspaces, mentorship networks, and vital seed funding resources, GUSEC bridges the massive gap separating student innovators from corporate reality. The platform enables non-tech as well as tech-driven dreamers to design impactful prototypes.

To date, the council has supported hundreds of startup initiatives, cultivated thousands of experimental minds, and nurtured sustainable employment paths across the state. GUSEC continues to redefine ecosystem rules by deploying inclusive business programs designed around an absolute zero-cost approach.

    Our Initiatives

    01

    herSTART

    herSTART is GUSEC's flagship initiative dedicated to empowering women entrepreneurs across India. Launched in 2022 by the Hon'ble President of India, the initiative has emerged as one of the country's leading platforms for supporting women-led innovation and entrepreneurship.

    Through a structured journey comprising bootcamps, accelerator programs, and demo days, herSTART provides aspiring and existing women entrepreneurs with access to mentorship, incubation support, funding opportunities, networking, industry connections, and a strong entrepreneurial community.

    Read More
    02

    Vikram Sarabhai Children Innovation Centre (VSCIC)

    The Vikram Sarabhai Children Innovation Centre (VSCIC) is a first-of-this-kind initiative by GUSEC, established in partnership with UNICEF, to nurture and support young innovators. The initiative provides dedicated incubation support, including mentorship, guidance, and prototyping assistance.

    By encouraging curiosity, creativity, and problem-solving, VSCIC empowers young minds to explore, create, and innovate while developing the confidence needed to become future changemakers.

    Read More
    03

    Institute of Innovation, Entrepreneurship and Skills (IIES)

    The Institute of Innovation, Entrepreneurship and Skills (IIES) is an initiative focused on developing innovation, entrepreneurship, and industry-relevant skills among students, professionals, aspiring entrepreneurs, and organizations.

    Through its diverse programs and expert-led learning experiences, IIES equips individuals with the knowledge, skills, and entrepreneurial mindset needed to thrive in a rapidly evolving world.

Who We Are

Building Bharat’s Innovation Infrastructure

Marwari Catalysts Group is a founder-first startup accelerator, venture studio and SEBI-registered AIF Category-I fund nurturing high-potential founders from India's Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.

We believe world-class innovation can emerge from every corner of Bharat. Through mentorship, acceleration programs, investor access, and ecosystem support, we help business scale from local ideas to global businesses.

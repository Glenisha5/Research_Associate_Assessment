About Amrita TBI

Amrita TBI is a non-profit startup incubator supported by Govt. of India and Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham that funds, mentors and nurtures ideas, startups and entrepreneurs. Virtual incubatees can operate from anywhere in India. Amrita TBI runs a scaled incubator program that incubates and accelerates startups that are in idea stages and growth stages. Amrita TBI also runs multiple programs such as Accelerator, PRAYAS for hardware startups and Entrepreneur-in-Residence with the objective to help startups scale nationally and internationally. Amrita TBI is looking for:

  • Early stage startups
  • Growth stage startups
  • Mentors to help our startups

In 2017, Amrita TBI won the National Award for the Best Startup Incubator in India. Award was conferred by the President of India

Amrita TBI is one of the select 6 incubators that will be made world class under AIM, NITI Aayog, Govt. of India.

Amrita TBI was also listed among the Top-5 most prolific startup incubators in India by the Economic Times in 2015. Amrita TBI has incubated 208 startups and mentored more than 500 startup ideas.

  • Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)

    created by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) with an outlay of Rs 945 Crore, aims to provide financial assistance to startups for proof of concept, prototype development, product trials, market entry and commercialization.

    Amrita TBI is a partner incubator with DPIIT to implement SISFS and disburse funds to selected startups.

    * Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs of investment for market entry/ commercialization/ scaling*.

    * Up to Rs. 20 Lakhs as a grant for validation of Proof of Concept/ prototype development/product trials.Startup selected. Slots filled

    Know More

  • ACCELERATOR

    Amrita TBI Accelerator is a fast paced program with a combination of the best practices to help early stage start-ups evolve into successful ventures. The startups can raise funding from invited external investors and Amrita TBI on the Demo Day. The total seed investment from Amrita TBI for the Top-3 startups is $100,000 USD.The startups Accelerator program is supported by Atal Innovation Mission, NITI Aayog and NSTEDB, Department of Science and Technology, Government of India.

    Amrita TBI Accelerator program brings in both national mentors and international mentors from Silicon Valley with varied experience across multiple domains and sectors. They have been executives at Fortune 500 companies, built startups and products with local and global appeal. The program allows idea validation, iteration, building of customer development models, enhancing the business models and building a go-to-market strategy. In the end, the startup should be fundable-ready by Demo-Day.

    Know More

  • Amrita TBI NIDHI - Entrepreneur-in-Residence (EIR)

    To inspire the best talents to be entrepreneurs, minimise the risk involved in pursuing start-ups, and to partially set off their opportunity costs of high paying jobs, NSTEDB has introduced NIDHI-Entrepreneurs-in-Residence EIR) Programme.

    Amrita Technology Business Incubator is one of the ten approved NIDHI-EIR centres by National Science & Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board (NSTEDB), Govt of India. Ten startup incubators are selected as Program Execution Partners under NIDHI-EIR program from all over India and Amrita TBI is selected from Kerala. The NIDHI-EIR Programme would provide subsistence grant upto INR 30,000 per month to an aspiring or budding entrepreneur of considerable potential for pursuing a promising technology business idea.

  • PitchFest

    If you have a great innovative idea or product and are looking for funding, Amrita TBI PitchFest is the perfect platform to showcase your ideas/products. PitchFest is a unique initiative by Amrita TBI to find the most promising startups of India.

    The PitchFest contest provides you a perfect platform to bring your idea to life. You get the opportunity to meet venture capitalists, CEOs and top C-level executives from around the world who can provide instant feedback to your ideas and business plans. You will also have the opportunity to network with these executives for further guidance. Startups that qualify for seed investment will receive mentorship in crucial areas for successful market penetration. Total seed investment outlay upto USD 200,000.

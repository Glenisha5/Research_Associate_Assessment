Ace Marketer Lauren Vaccarello & Peak XV’s Pieter Kemps on the Art of Brand Storytelling


Lauren Vaccarello, Chief Marketing Officer at WEKA, chats with Peak XV’s Pieter Kemps about her results-driven approach to marketing, digital campaigns, and brand storytelling. Drawing on her extensive experience at global enterprises like Salesforce, Lauren talks about how companies can create brand narratives, hiring strategies, and leverage AI as a resource.

She discusses the right time to announce a new product, setting monumental goals, and how to achieve them. This conversation was recorded during our Surge 09 US immersion in February 2024.

OUR PROGRAMS

We work side by side with founders to help them realise their ambitions. We distil our learnings and offer it to founders from advice and support to our community-based programs such as Surge, Spark, Build, Pathfinders and Guild.

Surge

A rapid scale-up program for early-stage startups in India and Southeast Asia

Spark

A mentorship and community-focused program for women founders in India and Southeast Asia

The Guild

A program and community for founding CEOs of successful growth-stage startups in our portfolio

Pathfinders

A platform connecting our founders to operators & investors who can help them succeed in new markets

PitStop

The largest annual investor and private company conference in India and Southeast Asia

Build

A program for growth-stage portfolio startups looking to turn rapid speed into sustainable growth

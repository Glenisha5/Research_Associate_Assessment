Launching Peak XV Partners

Our name has changed. Our conviction has deepened.



We are excited to share that Sequoia India & Southeast Asia (SEA) will rebrand to Peak XV Partners and will operate as a fully independent firm. Our new name, Peak XV was the original name given to Mount Everest. To us, it signifies the relentless pursuit of audacious goals by our founders, while overcoming challenges along the way. It’s a new beginning for us as Peak XV, but unlike most beginnings, this is an opportunity for us to build on top of the foundation laid over the last 17 years. Our firm will continue to be managed by the present leadership team and will continue to invest from the most recently raised set of funds focused on India and SEA.

Our Programs

We work side by side with founders to help them realise their ambitions. We distil our learnings and offer it to founders from advice and support to our community-based programs such as Surge, Spark, Build, Pathfinders and Guild.

Surge

A rapid scale-up program for early-stage startups in India and Southeast Asia

Spark

A mentorship and community-focused program for women founders in India and Southeast Asia

The Guild

A program and community for founding CEOs of successful growth-stage startups in our portfolio

Pathfinders

A platform connecting our founders to operators & investors who can help them succeed in new markets

Pitstop

The largest annual investor and private company conference in India and Southeast Asia

Build

A program for growth-stage portfolio startups looking to turn rapid speed into sustainable growth

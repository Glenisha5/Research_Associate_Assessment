A fresh vision for the future of classroom education.

The Present

Classrooms have a pedagogy that is reminiscent of the pre-internet era of teaching-learning through group lectures where one teacher speaks to a class of 60 students. There is no attention paid to the learning needs or styles of individual students and thus the quality of learning outcomes at leading public universities is a 67% failure rate.

The Future

Our vision for the classroom of tomorrow is to create a “Learner Centered Model” with high quality learning outcomes where nearly all students achieve learning objectives for the course. Through Connected Learning pedagogy, students choose between Self Learning, Peer Learning and Teacher assisted learning to suit their personal learning styles.

A vision backed by learning data

Our vision for the future of classroom education is based on data of learning styles of Indian students from our execution over the last 24 months along with Facebook School of Innovation and Freshworks School of SaaS at Sastra and GiTAM University.

Philosophy

“The aim of education / adhyayana (अध्ययन) is to be a tool for preparation for a life-long journey to awaken the primary faculties of human consciousness and become self-aware individuals.”

— India’s National Education Policy, 2020.

This journey of life-long education is depicted in our emblem of a child learning under the tree of awakening. The motto Anu Gacchata Pravaham (अनुगच्छत प्रवाहं) is a benevolent blessing for our pupils: “May you follow the universal flow of life”.

A full stack digital educational experience

By aligning stakeholders across all layers of the educational stack, we are accelerating the digital transformation of classroom education to a learner centered model.

Regulatory Layer

Pupilfirst is a founding member of the National Educational Alliance for Technology, a Public Private Partnership Scheme for promoting educational technology companies by Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Pedagogy Layer

We create our own Pupilfirst learning management system (LMS) for running an asynchronous online school, where learning is achieved through focused tasks, directed feedback, an iterative workflow, and community interaction.

Curriculum Layer

Along with industry partners like Facebook and Freshworks, we create competency based curriculums that delivers in-demand industry skills to students in emerging technologies.

Institutional Layer

The final magic ingredient is a methodological process that has been developed through two years of research that lets faculty guide students to achieve high quality learning outcomes consistently.

The project’s overall goal is to make modern tooling available in the field of education, and to do so in a manner that promotes effective techniques for improving student outcome.

Outcome driven teaching

Focus on creating real learning outcomes for students. Coach your students through an intensive ‘Learn by Doing’ process to achieve outcomes. (Jobs. Money. Degree.)

Give targeted feedback

You are an expert in your domain. Pupilfirst enables you to quickly and efficiently review the quality of task submissions by students, share feedback and create a conversation around what students have learned.

Foster a culture of sharing knowledge

Pupilfirst encourages collaboration by building student communities that can solve its own problems, and even supports teaming up of students to go through a challenging course together.
